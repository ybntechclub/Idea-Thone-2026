IDEA-THON 2026

Reimagining the Operating System for the Next Generation

Have you ever wondered why we still use operating systems designed around concepts created decades ago? What if you could forget Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux and design an OS completely from scratch?

Discover the Challenge Register Now

The Challenge

Design an idea for a new-generation operating system specifically designed for how people use mobile phones and laptops today—and how they may use them in the future.

Your OS can be completely different from anything that exists today. We are looking for ideas, not implementation.


You DO NOT need to:

  • ❌ Write code
  • ❌ Build an operating system
  • ❌ Develop an application
  • ❌ Create a working prototype

💡 You only need to think, imagine, and explain your idea.

Think Beyond...

AI Privacy Education Gaming Productivity Accessibility Security Communication Personalization Creativity Sustainability Offline Computing Device Integration

"If we were building an operating system from zero today, what would we build?"

What Should Your Idea Include?

01

OS Name

Give your operating system a unique and memorable name.

02

Your Vision

In a few sentences, explain what your operating system is fundamentally trying to achieve.

03

The Problem

What specific problem with today's operating systems are you trying to solve?

04

Your Innovation

What makes your OS fundamentally different from existing solutions like Android or Windows?

05

Key Features

Describe 3–5 major features that define your OS experience.

06

Mobile Experience

How would your OS work on smartphones? Think about interaction and UI.

07

Laptop Experience

How would the same OS adapt and work seamlessly on laptops?

08

Integration

Mobile ↔ Laptop: How would your phone and laptop communicate and work together?

09

The "Wow" Feature

What is the one feature that could make people say: "Why doesn't every computer work like this?"

Evaluation Criteria

Criteria
What We Look For
Originality
How unique is the idea?
Innovation
Does it introduce a genuinely new approach?
Problem Solving
Does it solve a meaningful problem?
Future Potential
Could this idea shape future computing?
Practical Thinking
Could the concept realistically become an OS?
Overall Concept
How strong and convincing is the idea?

One Last Question

"If my OS existed tomorrow, why would someone choose it instead of Android, iOS, Windows, or macOS?"

If you have a convincing answer—We want to hear your idea.

Submission Format

Keep your idea simple and clear. Recommended length: 1–2 pages.

Include: Team Name • Department & Semester • OS Name • Idea Description • Problem • Key Features • Mobile/Laptop Experience • Integration • Wow Feature
